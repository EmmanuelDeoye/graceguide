How to delete your account

In the app: open the menu → Settings → Delete Account, then confirm.

On this page: sign in below with the same account you use in GraceGuide, tick the box and tap Delete my account.

Can't sign in any more? Email support@graceguide.com.ng from the address on your account and we will delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. We delete:

  • Your login (email or Google sign-in) and your profile: name, photo, bio and Spirit Life.
  • Your Bible notes, highlights and bookmarks, study plans, devotionals and reading history.
  • Your conversations with Shepherd, your settings and your notification settings.
  • Your posts in Space and your Brethren connections.
  • Your direct message conversations. They are deleted for both people, so the other person no longer sees that chat either.
  • Your Weekly Quiz all-time record, your game profile (XP, wins, streaks and badges) and your game history.

What is kept

  • Messages you sent in forum groups, and comments you left on other people's posts, stay there for the other members, under the name you had.
  • Results already shown on past Weekly Quiz rounds and past daily game leaderboards.

Nothing else is kept. We do not keep backups of deleted accounts.
