Privacy Policy

GraceGuide · Effective 19 November 2026

Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Godled Tech, the publisher of GraceGuide on Google Play, collects, uses and protects your information when you use the GraceGuide Android app and the website at graceguide.com.ng (together, "GraceGuide").

Questions or requests: support@graceguide.com.ng.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and password (passwords are handled by Google Firebase and never seen by us), or your Google account name, email and photo if you sign in with Google.
  • Profile: username, profile photo, bio and the interests you choose.
  • Your study: Bible notes, highlights, bookmarks, study plans, reading history, devotionals and prayer or study streaks.
  • Shepherd: the questions you ask our AI assistant and its replies.
  • Community: Space posts, comments and Amens, direct messages, forum group messages, Brethren connections, and reports you make about other users.
  • Quiz and games: your answers, scores, XP, wins, streaks and badges.
  • Messages to us: anything you send through About Us or by email.

Information collected automatically

  • Activity: the days you use the app and when you were last active, used for streaks, Spirit Life and your Online / Away status.
  • Notifications: a push notification token for your device and your notification settings.
  • On your device only: preferences such as theme, Bible font size and devotional length are stored on your phone or browser.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos (other than a profile picture you choose), or advertising identifiers, and GraceGuide shows no ads.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and keep your data in sync across your devices.
  • To provide the features you use: Bible reading and notes, study plans, personalised daily devotionals, Shepherd, the Weekly Bible Quiz, games and community features.
  • To send the notifications you have switched on (reminders, messages, quiz alerts and announcements).
  • To keep the community safe: reviewing reports and removing content or accounts that break our guidelines.
  • To answer your messages and support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that run GraceGuide, and only what each one needs:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): sign-in, database, file storage for profile photos, and push notifications. Your GraceGuide data is stored here.
  • DeepSeek: produces Shepherd's answers and your personalised devotionals. What you type to Shepherd, and short context such as your recent reading topics, is sent to DeepSeek to create a reply. Please don't share sensitive personal details with Shepherd.
  • API.Bible: supplies Bible text. Only the passage being read is requested; no personal information is sent.
  • Video sites (YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, X, Instagram, Vimeo): when you play a video someone shared in Space, it loads from that site, which may collect information under its own privacy policy.

These providers may process data on servers outside your country. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users.

What other users can see

  • Your username, photo, bio, Spirit Life level (unless you set it to Brethren only), game wins and Online / Away status.
  • Your Space posts and comments, your forum messages, and your name and score on quiz and game leaderboards.
  • Direct messages are seen only by you and the person you are messaging.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users. To let people find you by email, we keep only a one-way hash of it, which cannot be turned back into your address.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your information for as long as you have a GraceGuide account. You can delete notes, highlights, posts and messages yourself at any time.

You can delete your whole account in the app (Settings → Delete Account) or on our account deletion page. Your account and data are deleted straight away, including your direct message conversations, which are removed for both people. Messages you sent in forum groups and comments on other people's posts stay for the other members. Full details are on the account deletion page.

Security

Data travels between your device and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and is stored with Google Firebase, protected by access rules so that your private data can be read only by your own account. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

GraceGuide is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • View and update your profile in the app at any time.
  • Turn notification categories on or off in Settings, or in your phone's settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it by emailing support@graceguide.com.ng. We reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change this Privacy Policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
